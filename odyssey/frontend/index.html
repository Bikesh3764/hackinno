Pipeline

  1. SenseElevation map, 6-band camera, heat, neutron and rover 3D points, all noisy.
  2. FuseMerge orbit and rover heights, fill gaps, keep an error bar per cell.
  3. PerceiveSlope, roughness and terrain type from a Random Forest, with uncertainty.
  4. RiskChance each cell is too steep, rough or rocky. Above 50 % is off-limits.
  5. CostHard, risky or uncertain ground costs more to cross.
  6. PlanA*, Dijkstra and RRT* find the cheapest safe route.
  7. DiscoverFind and rank science spots, including buried ice, with confidence ranges.
  8. ActDrive, spot new hazards, replan on board, verify predictions on site.

Data

SimulatedGenerated terrain with known truth. Real MarsHiRISE elevation of Jezero crater (USGS / Univ. of Arizona), 2 m cells. Labels4 % of pixels used for training; the rest for testing.
